Watchtower One

Unlock your vault

Your notes remain encrypted until your local vault opens. Nothing is sent to a Watchtower service.

Unlocking securely. This may take about five seconds.

Replace Recovery Secret

Authorize replacement with your current vault passphrase. The old Recovery Secret will stop working after confirmation.

Generating securely. This may take about five seconds.

Delete this local vault

This permanently retires its local encryption keys, then removes the encrypted profile from this device. It does not delete synced copies, backups, or guarantee physical erasure from an SSD.

Retiring the vault before removing its encrypted files.

Change vault passphrase

Confirm your current passphrase and choose a replacement. Your encrypted notes will not be rewritten.

Changing securely. This may take about five seconds.

Recover your vault

Enter your Recovery Secret and choose a new vault passphrase. Your existing notes will not be rewritten.

Recovering securely. This may take about five seconds.

Save your Recovery Secret

Keep this somewhere separate and safe. It can recover your vault if you forget the passphrase. Watchtower cannot recover it for you.
